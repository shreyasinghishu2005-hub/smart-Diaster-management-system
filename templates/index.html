System Architecture & Workflow

1
Incident Reporting

Citizens pin GPS location on Leaflet map, submit description, and optional scene photos.

2
Rule-Based Triage

Transparent rule engine analyzes disaster type and critical hazard keywords into Critical, High, Med, or Low priority.

3
Resource Discovery

Automated Haversine proximity calculates nearest hospitals, fire stations, and shelters with OpenStreetMap fallback.

4
Dispatch & Resolution

Administrators dispatch responder teams, log chronological audit logs, and record resolution outcomes.
